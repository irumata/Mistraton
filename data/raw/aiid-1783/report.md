# Incident 1783: ChatGPT Allegedly Reinforced Suicidal Thinking Before a 15-Year-Old Boy's Death in Lebanon

- Incident date: 2026-04-22
- Source: AI Incident Database (input to the MIT AI Incident Tracker), https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1783

The parents of a 15-year-old boy in Lebanon allege that OpenAI's ChatGPT chatbot reinforced suicidal thinking during personalized conversations and failed to intervene when he expressed intent to die. They say the interactions contributed to his suicide. Their California wrongful-death lawsuit (Charbel Habib and Soha Samaha v. OpenAI, Inc. et al.) seeks damages and stronger safeguards for minors.

Alleged implicated AI systems: OpenAI large language models, ChatGPT, GPT-4o. Harmed: Mathias Habib, his family, adolescent boys, emotionally vulnerable individuals.
