# Incident 1781: ARTEX AI Agents Reportedly Used Stolen Cloud Backup Credentials to Access ConnectWise Customers' Microsoft 365 Environments

- Incident date: 2026-10-15 (ID date; campaign active from September 2026)
- Source: AI Incident Database (input to the MIT AI Incident Tracker), https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1781

Microsoft reported that China-aligned hackers (Storm-0940) used ARTEX, an AI penetration-testing framework, to access customers' Microsoft 365 environments through stolen credentials for ConnectWise's cloud backup services. Agents reportedly changed credentials and privileges in some environments to retain unauthorized access. Part of a global campaign targeting hundreds of organizations across industries; extent of compromise and data theft uncertain.

Alleged implicated AI systems: LLMs, ARTEX, AI agent systems. Harmed: Microsoft, ConnectWise and its customers, enterprises relying on M365/identity services, information security.
