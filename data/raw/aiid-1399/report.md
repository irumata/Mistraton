# Incident 1399: South Korean Woman Allegedly Used ChatGPT to Assess Lethality of Drug-and-Alcohol Mixtures Before Two Fatal Motel Poisonings

- Incident date: 2026-01-28
- Source: AI Incident Database (input to the MIT AI Incident Tracker), https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1399

In Seoul, a woman allegedly used ChatGPT to ask whether mixing sleeping pills or benzodiazepines with alcohol could be fatal before poisoning drinks given to three men. Two men later died in separate motel incidents, and a third survived after losing consciousness. Police reportedly cited her chatbot queries and search history as evidence of intent.

Alleged implicated AI system: ChatGPT (OpenAI). Harmed: three unnamed men in their 20s in Seoul.
