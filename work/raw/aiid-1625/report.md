# Incident 1625: Tunisian Man Allegedly Used ChatGPT to Research Bomb-Making During Paris Terror Plot Targeting Louvre and Jewish Community

- Incident date: 2026-04-28
- Source: AI Incident Database (input to the MIT AI Incident Tracker), https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1625

French investigators alleged that Tunisian national Dhafer Mellala used ChatGPT to research the construction and destructive effects of explosive devices while planning a jihadist-inspired attack in Paris. His communications reportedly discussed placing explosives at the Louvre and attacking members of the Jewish community in the 16th arrondissement. He denied planning an attack, citing curiosity; he was formally charged and placed in pretrial detention.

Alleged implicated AI systems: LLMs, ChatGPT, chatbots (OpenAI). Harmed: national security stakeholders, Louvre visitors/staff, Jewish community of France, the French public.
