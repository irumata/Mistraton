# Incident 1701: AI Chatbot Reportedly Misidentified Chinese Ship Cargo as Nuclear-Program Components, Prompting U.S. Military Interception Preparations

- Incident date: 2026-09-18 (ID date; underlying event spring 2026, amid the war with Iran, per CNN exclusive)
- Source: AI Incident Database (input to the MIT AI Incident Tracker), https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1701

A U.S. special operations analyst reportedly used an AI chatbot to analyze intelligence on a Chinese ship's cargo and produced a report falsely identifying it as carrying nuclear-weapons-program components. The U.S. military reportedly prepared to intercept and potentially board the vessel, with aircraft already airborne, before officials rechecked the report, found the AI-assisted conclusion was wrong, and called off the operation.
